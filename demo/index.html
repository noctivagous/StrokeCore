StrokeCore — the “x” test + brush physics

Spines and tools, no stored outlines — every letterform is baked from its strokes each time a control moves. The x and H are rigid-nib work (Phase 1); the swell and the Roman A are brushes (Phase 2): pressure swells the pointed brush, and the flat brush twists through the A's stems. Compare the red ghost on the nib glyphs: the same spines through a conventional PostScript-style stroke.
